Now let’s look at the UI.
The Blue circular map in the middle is the radar, you can see enemies and points of interest on it.
The Yellow/orange gauge on the left is for your thrust, it tells you how fast you're flying or if you are going forwards or backwards.
The blue gauge on the left tells you how much cargo you have picked up.
The green bar on the right shows you the hull’s health.
The blue bar on the right shows you the ship’s shield health.
The red text shows you the weapon type you have equipped
You can change weapons using the mouse scroll wheel, and you can lock on to a target using right click, and shoot with left click
If you have a target locked, you will see that targets health and shield on the left side of the screen. Using the missiles requires a locked target.
Try to kill the enemy in front of you, lasers are good against shields and missiles are good against the hull.
That was it for the flight lesson, go through the yellow portal to end the tutorial after pressing "Enter".
